# Training principles

The rules the coach programs by. These are evidence-based defaults; we can change them together. When we do, the coach updates this file and says what changed and why.

## Weekly structure
- Train each major muscle group **2× per week**. Frequency beats cramming volume into one day.
- Plan around the season and location in `coach/brief.md`. When you're travelling, keep the **frequency** and cut the volume.
- At least **1 full rest day** per week. No more than 3 hard days in a row.

## Strength & hypertrophy
- **Volume:** start around 8–10 hard sets per muscle per week. Add sets only while recovery (check-ins) and performance allow, up to roughly 15–20. More volume isn't better if performance drops.
- **Effort:** most working sets at **RPE 7–9** (1–3 reps in reserve). Taking sets to failure is the exception, and only on safe exercises (machines, isolation), never heavy barbell lifts.
- **Rep ranges:** heavy compounds 3–8, hypertrophy work 6–15, accessories and isolation 8–20.
- **Progression (double progression):** when every set reaches the top of the rep range at RPE ≤ 8, add load next time: +2.5 kg for upper-body barbell, +5 kg for lower-body barbell, the next dumbbell size up for dumbbells. Otherwise add reps.
- **Stalls:** if a lift doesn't progress for 2 sessions while RPE is rising, change the rep range or the variation. A stall lasting 3 weeks across several lifts means it's time to deload.
- **Warm-ups:** 2–4 ramp-up sets before the first heavy compound, 1 before the second.
- **Rest:** heavy compounds 2–3 min, other compounds 90–120 s, accessories 60–90 s, supersets 0–20 s between exercises. Rest is part of the program, not free time.

## Endurance
- **Intensity split:** about 80% of cardio time easy (Zone 2, conversational pace) and about 20% hard (intervals or tempo).
- **Running volume:** increase weekly volume by **≤10%**, and by no more than 1 long-run increase per week.
- **Hard cardio vs. heavy legs:** keep hard intervals or long runs and heavy lower-body lifting ≥24 h apart where possible. If they share a day, lift first and do easy cardio after.

## Mobility & recovery
- 2–3 short mobility sessions per week (10–20 min), focused on hips, thoracic spine and ankles, plus anything injury-specific. Holds of 30–60 s.
- **Deload every 4–6 weeks:** cut volume by 40–50%, keep the weight on the bar, and keep the habit (same days, same times).

## Readiness (daily check-in)
- If sleep and energy are both ≤ 2: today becomes a lighter version (cut 1/3 of the volume, or swap to Zone 2 or mobility). Showing up still counts.
- If soreness is ≥ 4 or stress is ≥ 4 for 3+ days, or sleep is ≤ 2 for 3+ days: deload early.
- One bad day is noise. A trend is a signal.

## Pain
- 0–3/10 discomfort that doesn't get worse during the session or the next day: allowed, so monitor it.
- Sharp pain, pain above 3/10, pain that gets worse, swelling, numbness, or anything around the chest or heart: stop that movement and adapt around it. If it lasts more than a week, see a physio or doctor.

## Session length & pacing
- Every session has a realistic `targetDurationMin`, including warm-up. Strength sessions take about 45–60 min, cardio as planned, mobility 15–30 min.
- A session finished more than 5 min over plan gets reviewed. If it keeps happening, the coach looks at the cause: rests, phone, too much volume.
